Skedaddle

A packing game based on Jeannette Walls's The Glass Castle

Drag an item into the car, or tap it and then tap where you want it to go. R rotates whatever you are holding. You can also drag an item back out if you want to unpack it.

Calm

  • Soft music and no timer. Click on your things to make the place feel like home.
  • The Home meter goes up every time you unpack something.
  • You never know how long the calm is going to last.

Chaos

  • Dad (or the weather) gives you seconds instead of minutes.
  • Dad's things go in first, and yours have to fit around them.
  • Anything that doesn't make it gets stamped LEFT BEHIND.

The ledger

  • The game keeps a list of everything you lose.
  • You get to see it at the end, after the last chapter.
